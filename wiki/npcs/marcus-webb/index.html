    Marcus Webb¶

    Age 15 · Resident of the Hollow

    Overview¶

    "Everybody still treats me like a kid. I'd rather you just see what I can actually do."

    Marcus doesn't remember much about before — this world is the only one he really knows. Quick, restless, and always looking for a way to prove himself useful, he's easy to underestimate and hates being underestimated.

    He is the only child at the Hollow, and the only person there who has never known anything else. That makes him the one the others instinctively protect, and the one most likely to resent it.

    It also puts him at the sharp end of the strangest thing anyone in these mountains has reported: that a walker was once seen to pause before attacking a child. Nobody knows what that means. Nobody wants to find out using Marcus.

    Personality¶

    Curious, a little too bold for his own good, fiercely protective of the few things he has left from before.

    Looking For¶

    A friend, an older-sibling type, or someone willing to take him seriously. Not looking for anything romantic — just someone who'll actually listen when he has something to say, and maybe teach him something worth knowing.

    Relationships¶

    No survivor took Marcus as an Anchor. He and Tucker Vance are the two residents of the Hollow with no PC tie — which, for a fifteen-year-old who wants to be taken seriously, is its own kind of answer.

    Session History¶

    • Nine in the Hollow — introduced as one of the five already at the Hollow, and as the group's only minor. Unclaimed as an Anchor.
