# Dr. Charles Greenbriar

**Player:** Conner · **Archetype:** Scientist · **Drive:** "I'm not crazy"

## Overview

He introduces himself as **Dr. Charles Greenbriar**. He goes by **Chuck**.
Whether the doctorate is real is, in his own group's assessment, *dubious*.

What is not in dispute is that he trained as a biologist, and that what he
actually wanted to do with it was **cryptozoology**. He never got the chance —
and then the dead started walking, which he takes as vindication. He is convinced
the walkers connect to the thing he spent his career being laughed at for. There
is *something* about them. He is going to prove it.

His working theory, offered without hedging, is that the outbreak began **when
Bigfoot caught COVID**.

He came to Chattanooga for the aquarium. He met locals who told him about a
moonshiner up in the hills, went looking for him, and stayed at
[Dale Pruitt](../npcs/dale-pruitt.md)'s cabin until the two of them were forced
out into the wilderness. They walked until they found somebody else's cabin, and
that is how the Hollow got two more mouths.

## In Character

> "I'm not crazy."

> "They are real. It's a hundred percent right."

> "When Bigfoot got COVID — that's what happened."

**Drive** — *"I'm not crazy."* He made it up rather than take one off the list.

**Flaw** — morally flexible, and running on a delusion nobody at the Hollow has
the heart to puncture.

## Capabilities

- **Gear:** a firearm, a sledgehammer, and a portable gas stove with a lighter.
  The image the table settled on is a man waving a sledgehammer and a gun,
  insisting he is not crazy.
- No medicine. The group noticed. The group is not thrilled.

## Anchor

**[Dale Pruitt](../npcs/dale-pruitt.md)** — the moonshiner who took him in before
any of this. Chuck's read on the relationship is unusually honest: *"Dale sees
things when he's at the bottom of a bottle. He feeds my delusions."* It is a
genuine bond and a closed loop at the same time, and everyone else can see the
second part.

## The Best Searcher at the Hollow

He spent the morning of the crash checking small snares for small game, and one
large snare for Bigfoot. Then he went through a hundred and fifty yards of burning
wreckage and came out with a semi-automatic rifle, a case of money, and
**hospital-grade medical supplies** — more than the rest of the party found
between them. All those years of looking for something that isn't there appear to
have made him very good at finding things that are.

He also found the **IVY-4 case**: military-grade, and shattered. Vials broken,
samples contaminated or destroyed. A biologist, standing over the wreck of a
sealed sample container, five years into a plague nobody explained.

He got one intact document out of it. He has not shown it to the others.

He is also the man who called Tolliver's people over to the medical supplies
before anyone had agreed how the salvage was being split.
