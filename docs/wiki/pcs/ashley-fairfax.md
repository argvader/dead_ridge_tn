# Ashley Fairfax

**Player:** Graycen · **Archetype:** Nobody · **Issue:** Doesn't care if she lives · **Drive:** "Keeps going anyway"

## Overview

Ash was a college dropout and a burnout — smoked weed, didn't do much else, and
wasn't looking forward to the rat race she'd walked away from. She misses it now.
That's the joke of her, and it isn't funny to her.

She was **delivering a pizza** when it happened. Caught out in a herd mid-delivery,
she booked it into the woods and kept going, and she has been going ever since.
She stumbled onto the cabin at the end of the logging road the way she stumbles
onto most things. Of the four survivors at [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md),
she is the one everybody knows least.

She does not particularly care whether she lives. She has not stopped moving.
Both of those are true at once, and the second one is the only reason she's here.

## In Character

> "Wasn't looking forward to the rat race. Miss it now."

**Issue** — she doesn't really care if she lives.

**Drive** — *"Keeps going anyway."* Her drive loops straight back into her issue,
which is the point.

## Capabilities

- **Talent: Wallflower.** She doesn't take a single NPC as her Anchor — **the
  whole group is her anchor**. Her stated reason: *"So I don't get as sad if one
  of them dies."* It also means that if they all die, she has nothing left to
  steady her.
- **Gear:** a machete, a frying pan, and a fishing rod. *"We're packing light."*

## Anchor

**The whole group**, by way of the Wallflower talent — no single NPC. A hedge
against grief that is also a bet she can't win twice.

## Relationships

- **Johnathan Banks** — she showed up at his cabin and he let her stay. That's
  the whole of it so far.
- **Dr. Charles Greenbriar** — she may have called the cops on him back in
  Chattanooga, before everything. Neither of them has fully committed to that
  story.
- **Clara "CJ" James** — no history before the Hollow.
