# Johnathan Banks

**Player:** Ian · **Archetype:** Broker *(homebrew)* · **Drive:** "I just gotta ring the bell"

## Overview

Wall Street didn't end when the world did — not for Johnathan Banks. He still
talks in positions and upside, still reads a room like a spread, and still
believes, somewhere under all of it, that the market is coming back and he will
be standing there when the bell rings. He goes by **JB**, or **Johnny B**.
Someone floated "Banksy" once; he didn't take to it. *"I'm not into that art
stuff. The only art I see is stocks going up and down."*

Before the outbreak he ran insider trades for mobsters. He rather hopes they're
all dead. They probably aren't.

The cabin the survivors now live in is **his** — the Banks family place, his
father's or his grandfather's, at the end of a logging road nobody maintained
even when there was somebody to maintain it. He was a rich, snarky punk with a
hunting cabin he barely used, and he has spent the five years since taking in
stragglers against what he insists is his better judgment. He is not warm about
it. He keeps doing it anyway.

He carries no weapon. He carries **letters of introduction to three other
havens**, which he considers the better armament.

## In Character

> "I like the hockey stick. Up and to the right."

> "You're necessary for me to live."

> "Y'all are gonna hate me, but you'll need me."

**Drive** — get back to Wall Street. *"I just gotta ring the bell."*

**Flaw** — morally flexible, and cheerfully aware of it. He intends to be the
kind of man the group wants to kill and can't afford to.

## Capabilities

- **Key skill: Manipulation.** Empathy is his best attribute, and he spent his
  points on talking people into things.
- **Talent: Silver Tongue.**
- **Gear:** sorted small-value trade goods; a set of scales to weigh them; and
  letters of introduction to three other havens. He rolled the scales *twice* —
  *"in case one of them's off, I like to check my work."*
- **No weapon at all.**

## Anchor

**[Sister Ruth Okafor](../npcs/ruth-okafor.md)** — the hospice nurse. He met her
at the retirement home, one of the havens he holds a letter for, and talked her
into leaving with him: *"Just read these people their last rites and then come
help us."* He recruited her to the Hollow. He is entirely clear-eyed that this
was an awful thing to do and did it anyway.

## Relationships

- **Clara "CJ" James** — his neighbor from before, another wealthy household on
  the same street. When it fell apart he took her with him. She is the reason the
  cabin is still standing.
- **Dr. Charles Greenbriar** — arrived at the Hollow as a stranger, on foot, out
  of the woods, with [Dale Pruitt](../npcs/dale-pruitt.md) in tow.
- **Ashley Fairfax** — pure happenstance. She turned up. He let her stay.
