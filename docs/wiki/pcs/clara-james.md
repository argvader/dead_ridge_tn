# Clara James

**Player:** Cobie · **Archetype:** Homemaker · **Issue:** Bloodthirsty · **Drive:** Take down as many as possible

## Overview

**CJ** was a homemaker in a good neighborhood, the kind with pilates on the
calendar and a martial-arts class squeezed in between school runs and soccer
practice. She has no family now. What she has instead is a number she is trying
to run up.

She is the most dangerous person at [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md) and
everyone there knows it. When the group weighed taking the indefensible
Buc-ee's plaza, the argument against it was that they would all die — and the
argument for it was, essentially, *we've got CJ.* Somebody said they weren't
relying entirely on CJ. Nobody believed them.

She was **Johnathan Banks' neighbor** before the outbreak — another wealthy
household on the same street. He took her with him when it fell apart. It is
not clear which of them got the better end of that.

## In Character

> "Quick with that rolling pin. Or a knife."

**Issue** — Bloodthirsty.

**Drive** — take down as many as possible. She wants revenge for her family, and
the arithmetic of it is the closest thing she has to a plan.

## Capabilities

- **Favorite attribute: Agility**, taken to 5 — the highest number anyone at the
  Hollow put anywhere.
- Built for close combat. The table's image of her is a soccer mom with the
  training actually behind it.
- **Gear:** among the group's better hauls — a car, bone arrows, and a bag of
  medicinal herbs.

## Anchor

**[Birdie Coyle](../npcs/birdie-coyle.md)** — the former park ranger. They bonded
over a shared love of nature and **growing things**, which is the softest fact
established about CJ all session and sits at an odd angle to everything else
about her. They didn't know each other before; the bond was built at the Hollow.

It is worth noting what she chose. The most lethal person at the haven anchored
herself not to a fighter but to the woman who knows how to make things live.

## Relationships

- **Johnathan Banks** — her neighbor from before, and the man who got her out.
  The oldest tie in the group.
- **Dr. Charles Greenbriar** — he'd like to hide behind her, and says so.
- **[Birdie Coyle](../npcs/birdie-coyle.md)** — her Anchor, and her one
  established interest outside of killing.
- **Ashley Fairfax** — no history before the Hollow.

## Session History

- **[Nine in the Hollow](../../sessions/2026-08-30.md)** — created; established

  Anchor over a shared love of growing things. Her relationship with Banks —
  neighbors before, survivors after — is the oldest bond among the four.
