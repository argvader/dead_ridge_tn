# The Old Yeller

A school bus sitting where it stopped on a mountain road, yellow paint gone chalky under five years of weather, windows fogged from the inside. Everyone in this stretch of Tennessee gives directions by it. The story goes that the driver had a heart attack behind the wheel and turned with a full load of children still aboard — and that some of them are still on it. People say the bus is haunted. People also say you should give it room, which is the part they actually mean.

## The Story

The driver had a heart attack. Then he turned. The bus was full of kids.

That's all anyone tells you, and they tell it the same way every time, which is
usually a sign a story has been polished by a lot of retelling. Whether it
happened is a separate question from whether people believe it. Everybody
believes it.

**Some say the bus is haunted.** Nobody has volunteered to go and check.

## As A Landmark

It is the fixed point in a region where the maps stopped being useful — the thing
you navigate by, the thing you agree to meet at, the thing you tell a stranger to
look for. Which means everyone in these mountains, friend or otherwise, knows
exactly where it is.

It's also the turn for home. Coming back from the crash site at night, lost
in the dark with Chuck steering by the stars, the survivors saw a silhouette on
the ridge ahead in the moonlight: the bus. That meant the right turn back down
to [the Hollow](the-hollow.md). A haunted school bus full of dead children was
the most hopeful thing they saw all night.
