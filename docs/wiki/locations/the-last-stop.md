# The Last Stop

An unfinished travel plaza off the old mountain highway — fifty fuel pumps under an unlit canopy, a gift shop the size of a barn, and a fifteen-foot fiberglass beaver still waving at an interstate nobody drives anymore. It never got its grand opening. The dry-storage shelves are still stocked with jerky and candy, the walk-in coolers went warm years ago, and the whole plaza can be seen from miles off in every direction. There is enough floor space here to house a small army, which is roughly what it would take to hold it.

**Capacity 2 · Defense 1** — considered as a haven, passed over, and occupied by
somebody else.

## Who Holds It

Fifty people, five years on. They call themselves nothing in particular; the
survivors know them as [the Last Stop crew](../factions/the-last-stop-crew.md).
[Emmet Castellan](../npcs/emmet-castellan.md) leads them —
former special forces, brilliant in his lucid stretches and somewhere else
entirely in the others. [Wade Tolliver](../npcs/wade-tolliver.md) runs trade,
day-to-day operations, and anything that involves talking to outsiders.

There are sick people inside. Tolliver said so at
[the crash site](the-crash-site.md), asking for medicine, and did not say how
many or with what.

The plaza still has a pharmacy shelf. It is where Castellan gets his painkillers.

## Why They Didn't Take It

The survivors wanted it badly. Johnathan Banks in particular had designs on
being *King Buc-ee* and spreading the gospel of the beaver. The group talked
themselves out of it in about a minute: they would die horrible, horrible,
painful deaths, very quickly.

## Issues

- **Too big to lock down.** Between the fuel islands, the loading dock, and the
  shattered storefront windows, there are more ways in than anyone can watch.
- **Visible for miles.** The mascot and the sheer size of the plaza make it an
  obvious landmark — for travelers, raiders, and herds alike.
- **Bone-dry tanks.** The underground fuel was drained by looters years ago,
  which doesn't stop desperate people from turning up hoping to siphon something.
- **The propane cage out back is still full.** One stray round or grease fire and
  the whole plaza goes up.
