# Station Ivy

A name on a burned manifest and a set of coordinates pulled out of a madman's riddle. By [the Watchman](../npcs/the-watchman.md)'s numbers, Station Ivy is in the New River Valley of southwest Virginia, where the Appalachian ridgelines give out and the river runs north through the hills. It has power enough to keep samples at twenty below, fuel enough to fly them in, and a schedule. Nobody from [the Hollow](the-hollow.md) has seen it.

## What Is Known

The first pieces came from a clipboard held against a dead passenger's chest at
[the crash site](the-crash-site.md), and from eleven words spoken by a pilot who
died before she finished the twelfth.

The scorched fragment reads:

> *...DEP 0640 ...FUEL 40% REMAIN AT WAYPOINT... RLY STRIP 2 CONFIRMED OPEN...
> TOPPED OFF... 6 PAX + CARGO IVY-4... 0915 DEP RLY 2... HEADING E/SE...
> WEATHER DETERIORATING... LOW CEILING RPT'D AHEAD... ...STATION IVY EXPECTING
> ARR BY 1400 IF WX HOLDS...*

Her last words placed it: *"EAST near th—"*

From that:

- It **expected the aircraft by 1400**, which means it keeps a schedule, and
  someone there is waiting on an arrival that will never land.
- It was the **destination of the IVY-4 cargo**.
- It is reachable **by radio**, and the pilot's dying instruction was not to use
  one: *"Don't radio, they are listening."*

## What the Cargo Says

The document Chuck Greenbriar kept from the IVY-4 case, read aloud at the Hollow
in [The Half We Left Behind](../../sessions/2026-09-13.md), filled in what kind
of place it is:

- The coolers held **walker blood**, and had to be kept at **−20°C**. That takes
  power, and people who know how to keep it running.
- It is a **research site**. The work studies how the infection behaves, using
  **test subjects gathered from survivors out in the world**. Two of them were
  never exposed to walkers and turned anyway when they died.
- The notes mention **"improved suppression"** of the virus. They also appear
  to ask whether someone can be *made to turn without anyone laying a finger on
  them*.
- A second sample was hidden inside the plane's solar battery: a **control
  serum, untreated pool, reference sample only**. It is now sitting on a table at
  the Hollow with nothing keeping it cold.

Whether Ivy is looking for a cure or something else, Chuck is not convinced
either way.

## The Coordinates

Ash put a question out over a salvaged radio asking whether *IVY* meant anything
to anyone. [The Watchman](../npcs/the-watchman.md) answered with a riddle, and
worked out, it gave a location:

> *Where the ridgelines give out and the water starts downhill — thirty and seven,
> two past it, no further. Where the sun finishes its work and doesn't come back
> up from that side — eighty, and four past it, and stop.*

**37.2° N, 80.4° W.** That is the New River Valley in southwest Virginia, near
the West Virginia line and about a day's drive from the Hollow if nothing goes
wrong.

He ended with: *"Don't say her name on this line again."*

## Open Questions

- Who is expecting IVY-4, and what happens at Station Ivy when it doesn't arrive?
- The Watchman calls Ivy *her*, and treats the name as dangerous to say on the
  air. What does he know, and who did he think was listening?
- Ash said the name on the radio. Who else heard?
- The manifest gave a heading of *east-southeast* from the relay strip. The
  coordinates are northeast of the Hollow. Either the relay strip was somewhere
  else entirely, or somebody's directions are off.
- The plane came *out* of the **West**, carrying a passenger *out*, not in.
  There's a place worth fleeing at one end and a place worth flying five hours to
  at the other.
- Are Ivy's test subjects volunteers?
