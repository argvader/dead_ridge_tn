# Station Ivy

*A name on a burned manifest. Nobody has seen it.*

## What Is Known

Everything below comes from a clipboard held against a dead passenger's chest at
[the crash site](the-crash-site.md), and from eleven words spoken by a pilot who
died before she finished the twelfth.

The scorched fragment reads:

> *...DEP 0640 ...FUEL 40% REMAIN AT WAYPOINT... RLY STRIP 2 CONFIRMED OPEN...
> TOPPED OFF... 6 PAX + CARGO IVY-4... 0915 DEP RLY 2... HEADING E/SE...
> WEATHER DETERIORATING... LOW CEILING RPT'D AHEAD... ...STATION IVY EXPECTING
> ARR BY 1400 IF WX HOLDS...*

From that:

- It is **east or southeast** of these mountains — far enough that a Caravan
  needed most of five hours to reach it after a top-off.
- It **expected the aircraft by 1400**, which means it keeps a schedule, and
  means someone there is waiting on an arrival that will never land.
- It is the **destination of the IVY-4 case** — a sealed, military-grade sample
  container, now shattered on a Tennessee hillside.
- It is reachable **by radio**, and the pilot's dying instruction was not to use
  one: *"Don't radio, they are listening."* Whoever *they* are, they are not
  Station Ivy — or Station Ivy is not the only one on that frequency.

Her last words placed it: *"EAST near th—"*

## Open Questions

- Who is expecting IVY-4, and what happens at Station Ivy when it doesn't arrive?
- The plane was flying *out* of **West** and moving a passenger *out*, not in. A
  place worth fleeing at one end, a place worth flying five hours to at the other.
- Somebody is listening on the radio. Somebody was worth running from.
