# The Retirement Home

A retirement home somewhere off the mountain roads, still occupied five years on by the people who lived and worked there. The survivors inside built their barricade out of what they had: walkers. There are the aluminum kind with tennis balls on the feet, stacked and lashed across the doors, and by every account the other kind too. Johnathan Banks holds a letter of introduction that gets a door opened here, and it is where he found [Sister Ruth Okafor](../npcs/ruth-okafor.md) and talked her into leaving.

**A haven Banks has a letter for** · Not yet visited in play

## Who Is There

Nobody has been named. The people Ruth sat with are there, the ones Banks told
her to *"just read these people their last rites"* before she walked away to
[the Hollow](the-hollow.md). Presumably some of them are still alive.

## "Walkers on Walkers"

That's what the survivors at the Hollow call it. The joke writes itself, and
they kept telling it: residents without their dentures, stool softeners, pudding
cups that might still be sealed.

## Why They're Going

In [The Half We Left Behind](../../sessions/2026-09-13.md) the survivors put it
on the route to [Station Ivy](station-ivy.md) as the second stop, after
[the Last Stop](the-last-stop.md). The plan is a raid for **medicine ahead of the
winter**: old-people prescriptions, oxygen tanks, and whatever else a care
facility stockpiled.

The plan calls it a raid. Banks' letter says he's a guest. Ruth left the people
there to come to the Hollow. Nobody has worked out yet how those three things
fit together.
