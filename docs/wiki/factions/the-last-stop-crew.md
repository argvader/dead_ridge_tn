# The Last Stop Crew

**~50 people** · Holds [the Last Stop](../locations/the-last-stop.md) · Led by
[Emmet Castellan](../npcs/emmet-castellan.md)

## Overview

The community living in the unfinished travel plaza the survivors at
[the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md) looked at, wanted, and walked away from.
Where the survivors decided the place would kill them, this crew either didn't
get the luxury of that choice or made a different bet about the risk. Five years
on, there are fifty of them in there.

They are not raiders. When a working aircraft came down on the ridge, they
converged on the smoke for the same reason anyone would — because a plane
hasn't flown over these mountains in five years — and they were as thrown by it
as the party was.

## Leadership

- **[Emmet Castellan](../npcs/emmet-castellan.md)** — the man in charge. Former
  special forces, brilliant when lucid, self-medicating and unreliable when not.
  Referred to, only half in jest, as the king of the place.
- **[Wade Tolliver](../npcs/wade-tolliver.md)** — trade, outside relations, and
  day-to-day operations. The one who talks to strangers, and the only one in the
  court who does honest math before making a promise.

## Current State

- **Fifty strong**, in a structure the survivors judged impossible to hold. Either
  they have solved a problem the party couldn't, or they are living with it.
- **They have sick people.** Tolliver asked for the bulk of the crash's medical
  supplies on that basis, and did not elaborate on what the sickness is or how
  many it has.
- **Armed and disciplined.** Five of them came to the crash site; only three
  showed themselves at first. Nobody fired.
- **They negotiate.** Given first claim to a windfall they had every numerical
  advantage to simply take, they acknowledged the claim and asked instead.

## Relationship with the Survivors

Open, unsettled, and built on one conversation. Tolliver has an old tie to
Johnathan Banks — who once turned down a place with this crew — and used it. The
party gave up the hospital-grade medical supplies rather than fight over them,
which buys goodwill and establishes that the Hollow can be negotiated with. It
also tells fifty armed people where a smaller group's generosity begins.
