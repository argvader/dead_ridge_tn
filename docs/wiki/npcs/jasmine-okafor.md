# Jasmine Okafor

**Sister Ruth's niece** · Trainee pilot · *Possibly the pilot who died at [the crash site](../locations/the-crash-site.md)*

## Overview

Jasmine is the daughter of [Sister Ruth Okafor](ruth-okafor.md)'s brother. She was
nineteen when the outbreak hit and just getting started: working on her private
pilot's license, aiming to fly commercial one day, maybe for United. She was
living in Colorado. Ruth hasn't heard from her since.

Everything on this page comes from Ruth, at a campfire, on the night the
survivors came home from the crash.

## The Dog Tags

One of the two pilots in the wrecked cargo plane was a woman. She was still
alive when Johnathan Banks reached the cockpit. She told him not to use the
radio, that *they are listening*, that the samples had to reach Ivy, and then
she died. [Marcus Webb](marcus-webb.md) made sure she didn't come back.

Chuck Greenbriar took a pair of burned dog tags off the wreck. The only legible
letters were **O-K-A**. Ruth rubbed the metal with her thumb and found a **J**
in front of them. Banks described the woman to her.

*"That could be Jasmine,"* Ruth said. *"It sounds like Jasmine."*

## What Fits and What Doesn't

- **Fits:** the name. The plane was flying west to east, and Jasmine was out
  west. She was training to fly, and there aren't many pilots left.
- **Doesn't fit:** Jasmine was never in the military, and the pilot wore dog
  tags. She was training for airliners, and this was an old FedEx cargo plane.
- **Neither way:** five years is long enough for anyone to end up in a role they
  never trained for.
