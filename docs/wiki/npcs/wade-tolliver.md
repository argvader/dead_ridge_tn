# Wade Tolliver

**Trade and Outside Relations, [the Last Stop](../locations/the-last-stop.md)** · [The Last Stop Crew](../factions/the-last-stop-crew.md)

## Overview

Forties, weathered, and a dead ringer for Robert Duvall — the same quiet, tired
authority, the same habit of letting a silence do the threatening for him. He
carries a compact pistol with a red-dot sight, worn but well maintained, and he
almost never draws it. He doesn't need to. His negotiating table is the plaza's
actual defense system, and he knows it better than anyone there.

He runs day-to-day operations, trade, and outside relations for
[the Last Stop](../locations/the-last-stop.md). Among the people around
[Emmet Castellan](emmet-castellan.md), he is the only one who does honest math
before making a promise — which makes him the most reliable person in that
community and, depending on how you count, the most dangerous.

## Personality

Unhurried. Speaks less than the situation seems to call for and lets the gap sit
there. Doesn't posture, doesn't threaten, doesn't oversell — states what he wants
and what he'll trade for it, and means both. He arrived at the crash site with
five armed people at his back and opened by applauding.

## History with Banks

They knew each other before the outbreak, well enough that Tolliver has a name
for him: **"Johnny Bank Notes."** At some point after it, Johnathan Banks was
offered a place with the crew that now runs the Last Stop and turned it down.
Neither man has said out loud what that refusal cost or saved.

## Relationships

- **Johnathan Banks** — the older tie, and the reason the first meeting at the
  crash site didn't open with weapons. What sort of tie it is has not been said.
- **[Emmet Castellan](emmet-castellan.md)** — the man he answers to. Tolliver
  does the arithmetic Castellan doesn't always stay lucid enough to do.
- **The survivors at [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md)** — a first contact
  that ended in an accommodation rather than a fight, and then in a herd.
