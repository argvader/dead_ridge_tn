# Dale Pruitt

**Hill-Country Moonshiner** · Resident of [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md) · Anchor to Dr. Charles Greenbriar

## Overview

> "Come on by the still sometime. Bring your own cup."

Slow to trust, quick with a joke once he does. Dale's got a face that scares
strangers off before he's said a word, and a heart that gives the lie to it about
thirty seconds later. He knows this land, knows people, and knows exactly how to
talk his way through a bad situation.

He is the reason Chuck Greenbriar is alive. Chuck came to Chattanooga for the
aquarium, heard from locals about a moonshiner up in the hills, and went looking.
He stayed at Dale's cabin until the two of them were driven out into the
wilderness, and they walked together until they found the Hollow.

## Personality

Loyal once you're in, proud of what he's built, folksy sense of humor, generous
to a fault with people he likes.

## Looking For

Someone who'll sit on the porch a while and not need the silence filled.

## Relationships

- **Dr. Charles Greenbriar** — his Anchor, and the man he took in before any of
  this. Chuck's description of the bond is blunt: *"Dale sees things when he's at
  the bottom of a bottle. He feeds my delusions."* Whether Dale is humoring him,
  believes it too, or has simply never had a reason to argue is an open question.
