# Emmet Castellan

**Leader of [the Last Stop](../locations/the-last-stop.md)** · [The Last Stop Crew](../factions/the-last-stop-crew.md) · *Not yet met*

## Overview

The man fifty people at the unfinished travel plaza answer to. Former special
forces; smart, dangerous, and — by the account of his own second — unstable.

When he is lucid he is genuinely brilliant. He reads terrain and people
instantly, and he protects his own with a ferocity nobody who has met him
doubts. The trouble is the in-between. He self-medicates with whatever
painkillers are still on the plaza's pharmacy shelf, and he cycles: stretches of
clarity, then stretches where he is somewhere else entirely. Nobody at the Last
Stop appears to have solved for which stretch they'll get.

Everything known about him so far comes secondhand, from
[Wade Tolliver](wade-tolliver.md), who volunteered it without being asked. That
is either candor or a warning, and it may be both.

## The King of Buc-ee's

The survivors at [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md) considered the plaza as
a haven in their first days and talked themselves out of it, and Johnathan Banks
never stopped mourning the throne he didn't take. Somebody else took it. The
joke about being *King Buc-ee* has a real man sitting in the chair now, and by
every report he is not funny.

## Relationships

- **[Wade Tolliver](wade-tolliver.md)** — his negotiator and the one who does the
  honest math on his behalf.
- **Johnathan Banks** — Banks was offered a place with this crew once and
  declined it. Whether Castellan remembers that, or how, is unknown.
