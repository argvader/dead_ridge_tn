# Marcus Webb

**Age 15** · Resident of [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md)

## Overview

> "Everybody still treats me like a kid. I'd rather you just see what I can actually do."

Marcus doesn't remember much about *before* — this world is the only one he
really knows. Quick, restless, and always looking for a way to prove himself
useful, he's easy to underestimate and hates being underestimated.

He is the only child at the Hollow, and the only person there who has never known
anything else. That makes him the one the others instinctively protect, and the
one most likely to resent it.

It also puts him at the sharp end of the strangest thing anyone in these
mountains has reported: that a walker was once seen to **pause before attacking a
child**. Nobody knows what that means. Nobody wants to find out using Marcus.

## Personality

Curious, a little too bold for his own good, fiercely protective of the few
things he has left from before.

## Looking For

A friend, an older-sibling type, or someone willing to take him seriously. Not
looking for anything romantic — just someone who'll actually listen when he has
something to say, and maybe teach him something worth knowing.

## The Cockpit

He begged to come along to [the crash site](../locations/the-crash-site.md) and
Johnathan Banks said no. Clara "CJ" James overruled it — *he's got to learn
sometime, the days of babying are over* — and Banks conceded on the condition
that Marcus was CJ's responsibility. He got what he asked for.

What he got was Banks calling him into the wrecked cockpit, telling him to take
out his hatchet, and waiting while he worked out why. The dead have to be dealt
with this way or they come back. Banks talked him through it the way you talk
someone through a thing you have already decided they are going to do. Marcus
raised the hatchet, closed his eyes, and brought it down on the head of the dead
pilot. Banks handled the other one.

He came out of the cockpit with a blank face and the hatchet hanging bloody at
his side. Nobody at the Hollow had ever taken him seriously before. Somebody
finally did.

## Braveheart

Then the herd came, and Marcus froze. He stood by the cockpit with the pilot's
blood drying on his hatchet and didn't move. When CJ reached him and tried to
talk him out of it, all he could say was *"I just killed somebody… and now
zombies."* She picked him up and carried him over her shoulder.

A few minutes later he snapped out of it all at once. He fought his way out of
her arms, took the hatchet in both hands, charged the nearest walker, and killed
it. Then he ran for Banks like a kid who thought he was in *Braveheart*.

It didn't go perfectly. When Banks slid down the ravine, Marcus grabbed for him
and pulled the rest of the group down too. But at the bottom he had himself
together. He put the hatchet away and helped haul the wounded Banks up the roots
and out.

At the fire back home, CJ said he'd earned a drink and Banks agreed. He handed
Marcus the moonshine jug, and Marcus got it down through gritted teeth. *It's a
good burn, Marcus.*

CJ is already talking about taking him into the woods to find a couple of
walkers for practice.

## Relationships

No survivor took Marcus as an Anchor. He and [Tucker Vance](tucker-vance.md) are
the two residents of the Hollow with no PC tie — which, for a fifteen-year-old
who wants to be taken seriously, is its own kind of answer.

Since the crash, two of the four have a claim on what happened to him:
**CJ**, who insisted he was old enough to come and took formal responsibility for
him, and **Banks**, who refused him and then handed him the hatchet anyway.
