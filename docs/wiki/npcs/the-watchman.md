# The Watchman

**Voice on the wrong frequency** · Location unknown

## Overview

Tune a little too far past where you meant to stop and you might find him: a man
who calls himself **the Watchman**, after the graphic novel, which he took very
seriously. He talks conspiracy — the chemicals in the water, what they really put
in it, what it's turning people into. He is deep in the Kool-Aid and he is
extremely confident.

The important thing about him is not what he says. It's that **the broadcasts
keep changing**. It isn't a loop and it isn't a recording playing to an empty
room. Every so often there's a new one, which means that somewhere out there, five
years in, the Watchman is still alive and still transmitting.

Nobody knows where he broadcasts from. Nobody has met him. One day the broadcasts
will stop, and everyone who has been listening will know exactly what that means.

## What He Claims

- It's the chemicals in the water. It's the fluoride. That's what's turning them.
- Whatever else he's said, somebody out here has repeated it.

## He Answered

Ash got a salvaged radio working at the Hollow and put out a question: did *IVY*
mean anything to anyone? A few minutes later, the Watchman answered her.

> *Last time on this name. Everything out here runs backward. Once the sun's
> down, smoke goes back on the fire, deer walk their tracks in reverse, and so
> will I. Where the ridgelines give out and the water starts downhill — thirty
> and seven, two past it, no further. Where the sun finishes its work and doesn't
> come back up from that side — eighty, and four past it, and stop. Don't come
> looking for company. Don't say her name on this line again.*

Ash's review: *that Watchman guy speaks in riddles.* The survivors worked it out
anyway. The numbers are coordinates, **37.2° N, 80.4° W**, which puts
[Station Ivy](../locations/station-ivy.md) in the New River Valley of southwest
Virginia.

What stands out isn't the riddle. A man who broadcasts every conspiracy he can
think of went out of his way to hide this one answer, and ended it with a
warning. He calls Ivy *her*. He said this was the *last time* he'd talk about it.
He told them not to come looking for company, and he told Ash never to say the
name on the air again.

Chuck's reaction, when someone suggested the riddle was about a wendigo, was
that the Watchman hasn't seen anything but the bottom of a bottle. Someone else
at the fire said he sounded like a wise man.

## Relationships

He is still a voice, not a person. But he has now spoken directly to someone at
[the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md), which means he knows someone there is
asking about Ivy. The survivors had already noted that a man who never stops
talking on an open frequency is a man who can be found. The reverse is true too.
