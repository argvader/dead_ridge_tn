# The Watchman

**Voice on the wrong frequency** · Location unknown

## Overview

Tune a little too far past where you meant to stop and you might find him: a man
who calls himself **the Watchman**, after the graphic novel, which he took very
seriously. He talks conspiracy — the chemicals in the water, what they really put
in it, what it's turning people into. He is deep in the Kool-Aid and he is
extremely confident.

The important thing about him is not what he says. It's that **the broadcasts
keep changing**. It isn't a loop and it isn't a recording playing to an empty
room. Every so often there's a new one, which means that somewhere out there, five
years in, the Watchman is still alive and still transmitting.

Nobody knows where he broadcasts from. Nobody has met him. One day the broadcasts
will stop, and everyone who has been listening will know exactly what that means.

## What He Claims

- It's the chemicals in the water. It's the fluoride. That's what's turning them.
- Whatever else he's said, somebody out here has repeated it.

## Relationships

None established. He is a voice, not yet a person — though the survivors have
noted that a man who never stops talking on an open frequency is a man who can be
found.
