# Wanda "Birdie" Coyle

**Former Smoky Mountains Park Ranger** · Resident of [the Hollow](../locations/the-hollow.md) · Anchor to Clara "CJ" James

## Overview

> "I know these mountains better than I know most people, and I'm at peace with that."

Sharp-eyed and steady under pressure, Birdie spent years reading these ridgelines
long before the world fell apart, and she hasn't stopped since. She doesn't say
much until she's decided you're worth the words — but once she has, she'll walk
into the dark for you without blinking.

Of the nine people at the Hollow, she is the one whose knowledge the group is
most likely to need and least likely to ask for. If anyone can tell them how a
herd gets **up a mountainside**, it's the ranger.

## Personality

Watchful, dry sense of humor, fiercely protective, more sentimental than she'd
ever admit.

## Looking For

Someone who keeps their word, and doesn't need everything spelled out twice.

## Capabilities

Survival. She reads terrain, weather, and animal sign the way other people read
a room.

## Relationships

- **Clara "CJ" James** — her Anchor. They bonded over a shared love of nature and
  **growing things**, a tie built at the Hollow rather than before it. Birdie
  keeps the mountains alive in her head; CJ is trying to grow something out of a
  root cellar that's nearly bare. It is the gentlest relationship in the group,
  and it belongs to its most dangerous member.
